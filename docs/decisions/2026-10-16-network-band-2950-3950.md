# The network band is 2950-3950 Hz: bins 2950 / 3200 / 3450 / 3700 / 3950

Status: decided.
Date: 2026-10-16 (evening).

## Decision

The shared detection band (DET-013, `detection.band_low_hz/high_hz` on the Node, `SEQ band=` on the
Analyzer) is 2950-3950 Hz. With the 250 Hz bank spacing (16 kHz / 64) its bins are 2950, 3200, 3450,
3700 and 3950 Hz. The Node's hop train uses these five bins; the plain 3,200 Hz chirp stays and sits on
the second bin.

## Why

The host replay of the 2026-10-16 corpus (lab note section 9) showed the 3,200 Hz chirp off-bin in the
2800-3800 band that the batteries used (bins 2800 + k x 250): its energy splits over 3050 and 3300 and
the contrast P75 falls below the 30,500 gate in all 12 captures at every spacing, while on-bin tones pass.
A band-mode node would not accept idle, SELFTEST or 5a-reference chirps. Shifting the band keeps the
chirp frequency decision (`2026-10-09-chirp-frequency-stays-3200.md`) and the 5a reference intact; the
hop train only moves its bins. 3,950 Hz is inside the range the 2026-10-09 sweep found stronger, not
weaker, than 3,200.

## Rules out

- Changing `CHIRP_FREQUENCY_HZ` to land on a 2800-based grid.
- Hop steps off the 2950 + k x 250 grid (off-bin steps collapse the contrast; 2026-10-16 hopshape-60cm).

## Revisit when

The bench shows the new bins losing against the comb more often than the old ones (the 2800-3800 battery
is the comparison: hop5 25x5 gap 10 >= 9/10 at six spacings), or the band spacing changes (window size,
sample rate), which moves every bin.
