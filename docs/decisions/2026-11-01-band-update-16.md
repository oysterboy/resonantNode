# The band bank recomputes every 16 samples: half the Node's CPU for ~0.5 dB at the margin's edge

Status: decided (owner 2026-11-01, "1"; DET-020, step 22)
Date: 2026-11-01

## Decision

FreqBandStream recomputes the band bank every 16 samples (1 ms at 16 kHz), not every 4 (FREQBAND_UPDATE_EVERY_SAMPLES,
the Analyzer's SEQ default follows). The coverage minimum and every other detection value stay as they were; the
small loss at the margin's very edge is accepted.

## Why

- The Node's per-sample path: 34.6 us at 4, 15.7 us at 16 (55 % -> 25 % of the 62.5 us a sample allows; the longest
  block 4.5 -> 2.0 ms; bench/sessions/2026-11-01-step22.1-node-cpu). One compute on the 8-bin band costs ~108 us
  (2026-10-31-band-update-board); at 4 the bank was the largest CPU item, with Whispernet and ten nodes ahead.
- The replay keeps every verdict (2026-11-01-step22.2-update16). On the board, interleaved with 4: equal from the
  operating point to -6 dB, on both hop-loss cases, the chord cell and the music file; at -7.5 dB 43 / 50 against
  50 / 50, all coverage_too_low (74-76 ms against the 80 minimum) (2026-11-01-step22.3-edges).

## Rules out

- Lowering the coverage minimum to win back the edge, without its own tuning pass and noise check.
- Going back to 4 for detection reasons unless a room shows losses short of the edge.

## Revisit when

- The tryout room (step 15 / 18) reads margins near -7.5 dB on real pairs: then 8, or the coverage minimum, as a
  tuning pass (DET-019 is the same question from the amp side).
