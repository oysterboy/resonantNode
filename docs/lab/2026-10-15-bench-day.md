- Boards: E2 (MAC 24:dc:c3:4b:4c:e8) is the listener, E3 (24:dc:c3:49:b7:38) the emitter. COM ports moved between
  days (10-14: E2 = COM3, E3 = COM4; 10-15: E2 = COM5, E3 = COM3); the MAC identifies a board, not the port.
- Both boards are D-AMP, mic and speaker facing up on the bench, status LED on GPIO 2 fitted (step 4a closed).
- Emitter tone level 0.5 FS (`-D I2S_TONE_AMPLITUDE=0.5f`) except where a session says 0.3. Chirp 3,200 Hz, 100 ms.
- Preprocessing: First Difference up to the filter A/B on 10-14, then the DC blocker (default since `bfbe0c8`).
- Room: window and doors closed, Roomba off for the quiet runs; no temperature or room notes were taken
  (the owner added outdoor noise and the Roomba on 10-14 as they happened). Single runs, 10 trials per cell unless
  stated (50 for batteries); small counts are not rates.
- Detection: `TonalPulseScalar`, Analyzer builds, `SEQ start ... mode=detail`. "Expected" = detected in the trial
  window, "rejected" = detector or evaluator rejected, `amp_class`/`contrast_class` = the two inspectors.
- False answers: `SEQ OBS` silent windows (50 x 1.8 s, `period=2000 window=1800 report_settle=200`) with a radio
  50 cm from E2 at full volume, 90 degrees to the mic: WDR5 speech, then drum and bass.

## 2026-10-15: pulse trains, hop, band bank, gate battery

### Instrument checks
- Band stream cost (`FREQBAND_ENABLE_PROFILING`): 14.7 us per sample, 23.5 % of the 62.5 us sample period; Goertzel ~8 us per
  bin; room for a bank of ~8 bins. `2026-10-15-freqband-profile`.
- "clipped" in the Analyzer was never ADC clipping: the limits were 16-bit-era (lab note `notes_lab.md` 2026-10-15).
  Measured peak sample 1.1 % of 24-bit full scale at 3,200 Hz and 7 % at 800 Hz. The loud-end limits were scaled x256
  (`38c9bdb`); `RAW_AUDIO_HEALTH` reads `ok` again. `2026-10-15-clipfix-check`. `Strength16` saturates at ~3 % of full scale.
- Placement after the re-plug: 90 cm passes at 3,200 and 3,300 Hz (10/10 each). `2026-10-15-placement-90cm`.

### Pulse trains (DET-011), 3,300 Hz, 90 cm, 10 trials
- Default params: 15 ms pulses with 5 or 20 ms gaps are accepted 10/10 (136 ms event for gap 20: island 31 ms, largest
  gap 7 ms: the speaker/room tail fills ~13 ms of a 20 ms gap). 60 and 115 ms gaps split the train; 10 ms pulses 0/10.
  `2026-10-15-pulses-timing`.
- A gap is bridged only if the release debounce exceeds it; then the inspector runs after the debounce and the peak is older
  than the 256-bin FeatureHistory, so the classes come out "unknown". With 512 bins (`-D FEATURE_HISTORY_BINS=512`,
  Analyzer RAM +18.6 KB) 15 ms x3 with 60 ms gaps is 10/10; 115 ms gaps give amp class weak. `2026-10-15-pulses-wide`,
  `-pulses-hist512`.
- The permissive params (debounce 130, max gap 130, max duration 600, coverage 40) add no false answers on the single 3,300 Hz
  bin (speech 0, music 0; defaults 1, 0). `2026-10-15-trainparams-noise`. At a 2,000 Hz target the 20 ms-gap train passes, the
  60 ms-gap train merges but is rejected. `2026-10-15-trainparams-2000`.

### Hop (one frequency per pulse) and the band bank (DET-013)
- Emitter `PULSES ... hop=F1,F2,...` plays a frequency per pulse. With 500 Hz steps a fixed-band detector sees one pulse per
  frequency; 100 Hz steps leak into each other (bin width ~250 Hz). `2026-10-15-hop-check`.
- Band bank (`SEQ band=2800-3800`): FrequencyTarget = strongest of five 250 Hz bins; the plain chirp is unchanged
  (10/10 strong, 165 vs 164 ms) and a 3-frequency hop is read as one signal (10/10). Band with default params: 0 false
  answers on speech and music; band plus the wide train params: 4 on speech. `2026-10-15-band-bank`, `-band-noise`.
- Which scalar params accept a hop: a stepped hop of 30 ms pulses with 10-20 ms gaps passes with the defaults; sparse
  hops (15 ms pulses, 60 ms gaps) are held back by the amp class. `2026-10-15-hop-params`.

inspector (`AmpMagnitude`, P75 over the first 100 ms after onset, medium limit 2,500 on the profile) is calibrated for a
continuous chirp.

### Amp medium limit experiment
- Lowering the limit to 1,500 (`-D SCALAR_AMP_MEDIUM_THRESHOLD=1500.0f`): at 110 cm the 5-step hop goes from 0/10 to 10/10
  (gap 5) and 6/10 to 10/10 (gap 15), plain chirp unchanged. `2026-10-15-ampthr1500-110cm`.
- The price: false answers per 50 windows with the band, default params: limit 2,500 speech 0 / music 0; limit 1,500 speech 2 /
  music 18 (36 %). `2026-10-15-ampthr1500-noise`. The limit is what keeps music out; a smarter amp criterion for hops
  is needed instead of a lower number.

## Caveats the report should carry

- One room, one pair of boards, a few days; the comb moved between 10-14 and 10-15 (the 3,200 Hz chirp failed at 70/80 cm
  yesterday and passed today), so a pass/fail at one spacing is not a property of that spacing.
- 10 trials per cell: a single miss is 10 percentage points. False answers are single 50-window runs with different radio content.
- The Node build was only run for the DC blocker (10-14), not with hop or band; the band, hop and `FEATURE_HISTORY_BINS` live in
  the Analyzer path (hop playback is Emitter-side, built into the Node's tone output but not used by Behavior).
- The Analyzer build for most of 10-15 was flashed from uncommitted working copies right before commit; the logs carry the
  banner hash of the previous commit (`b309e96`/`b22b035`/`38c9bdb`) - the commit that holds the code is named per section above.
