## 2. Reproducible test audio and level matching

Tools (all on `main`, pure python, run with the PlatformIO interpreter; mp3 needs a venv with `miniaudio`):
- `tools/bench/buildaudio.py`: assembles one WAV from clips: 10 s silence, 100 s per section at equal RMS,
  5 s silences between, 10 s tail; writes a section list for `inspectstats.py --sections`. Options
  `--peak-safe`, `--hp TITLE=HZ` (high-pass), `--bass-db TITLE=DB` (add the part below the corner back).
- `tools/bench/synthtraffic.py`: synthetic traffic (rumble, roll noise, engine harmonics, horns, bell).
- `tools/bench/ambientlevel.py`: ambient broadband and band (2800-3800 Hz) level from RAW i2s captures,
  to match a playback level to a reference by band energy instead of by ear.
- `tools/bench/inspectstats.py`: per-file or per-section amp/contrast statistics, threshold counts.

Files (in the owner's Downloads, not in the repo; sources listed in `tools/bench/bench_audio.sections.txt`):
`bench_audio.wav` (music drum and bass from a 24-bit mix, speech female and male from LibriVox-style mp3s,
"tonal" flute from a vinyl rip, -24 dBFS RMS), `bench_audio_p6.wav` (same, +2.5 to +6 dB per section,
peak-limited), `bench_highway.wav` (freesound 873589, 150 s, high-passed 150 Hz), `bench_highway_bass.wav`
(bass added back at -1.2 dB re natural, peak-limited), `bench_highway_bass2.wav` (band part 9 dB lower, bass
+9 dB), `bench_traffic.wav` (synthetic).

## 5. Gate battery with the combined build (contrast 30,500, amp 1,500), 60-110 cm

Sessions `2026-10-16-gate-combined-{110,100,90,80,70,60}cm`, each with a README and the four cells.
Expected results of 10:

| Signal | 110 | 100 | 90 | 80 | 70 | 60 | spacings >= 9/10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| plain chirp 3,200 Hz | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | **0** | 10 | 5 of 6 |
| 3-step hop 30 x 3, gap 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | **6** | 5 of 6 |
| 5-step hop 20 x 5, gap 5 | 10 | 10 | 10 | **7** | 10 | 10 | 5 of 6 |
| 5-step hop 20 x 5, gap 15 | **1** | 10 | 10 | **0** | 10 | 10 | 4 of 6 |

2026-10-15 for comparison (amp 2,500, contrast 20,000): chirp 3,10,10,9,0,10; 3-step 10,10,5,0,10,10;
5-step gap 15 9,10,0,10,4,6; 5-step gap 5 0,10,10,10,10,0 (60...110 order there; see that note).

Findings:
1. **The inspector failures are gone.** On the 15th the hop failures were amp-class rejections at the highest
   strengths of the day. Today no trial of the chirp, the 3-step hop or the gap-5 hop fails at an inspector.
2. **The remaining failures are detector-side, a step in a comb null.** 80 cm gap 5 (3 rejects): the detector
   sees 79 ms / 77 ms coverage, two of five steps missing. 60 cm 3-step (4 rejects): 58 ms, one of three
   steps missing, the rest below `min_duration` 85 ms. A shape that still clears 85 ms with one step missing
   is the lever (section 6).
3. **Gap 15 is not acceptable with this criterion.** At 110 and 80 cm the detector sees a truncated train
   (88-97 ms) and the occurrences that reach the inspectors have contrast P75 about 29,800-30,100, below
   30,500: P75 over the first 100 ms with 15 ms gaps inside is lower than for a dense train. Needs a contrast
   criterion that ignores gaps (sub-window or span), or a tighter train.
4. **The comb moved again.** Chirp amp at 110 cm 2,042-2,427 today against 3,812-4,520 on the 15th; chirp at
   70 cm 10/10 on the 15th, 0/10 today (detector sees 24-25 ms of 100 ms: 3,200 Hz in a null). The 5a
   reference (chirp at 90 cm) is unchanged at 10/10.
5. Contrast margins of passing hops: typically 31,500-32,700; the smallest 30,968 (80 cm, gap 5).
   No dropped buffers in any cell.

## 6. Hop shapes at 60 cm (session `2026-10-16-hopshape-60cm`)

| Cell | result | contrast P75 |
|---|---|---|
| 4-step 30 x 4, gap 10, hop=2800,3133,3467,3800 | **0/10** | 28,330-29,241 (weak), 3 duration_too_long |
| 5-step 25 x 5, gap 10, hop=2800,3050,3300,3550,3800 | 10/10 | 30,989-31,529 |

**Hop frequencies must sit on the band-bank bins** (250 Hz spacing from the band's low edge): 3,133 and
3,467 Hz fall between bins, the peak bin is off-centre for two of four steps and the contrast collapses. A
design rule for the Node hop (BEH-005 / OUT-005), not a detector fault. The 25 x 5 gap 10 train passes at
60 cm with the smallest margin of the day and is untested at the other five spacings; one missing step leaves
about 130 ms, so it is the candidate for the "step in a null" failure.

## Caveats for the report

- One room, one day, 10 trials per cell, single passes for every noise run. The comb moves day to day, so
  the per-spacing pattern is not reproducible; the aggregate ("no single shape passes everywhere, hop
  failures are detector-side") is the finding.
- Radio content differs every run; file material is reproducible but (at matched band level) a weaker
  stressor than the phone radio. Reasons are hypotheses (speaker spectrum).
- The contrast limit 30,500 rests on 27 false-answer occurrences from two days and the hop values at six
  spacings; the gap to the hops is 400-1,000 at the worst cells, and one 2026-10-15 music occurrence
  (31,223) is above it.
- Both builds on E2 today are experimental overrides; the banners say `162c847`/`b22b035` but the behaviour
  is not the repo default.
