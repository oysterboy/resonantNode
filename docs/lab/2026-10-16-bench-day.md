Code only, every env compile-verified, nothing flashed (board state as in section 8):

  history 512 are the defaults (the battery's build); decision file decided. Noise OBS with max duration 300

  band bins for heard-pattern responses, start bin from the MAC (`output.hop_rotation`), own-emit suppression
  over the train; `RB signal ...` at boot, band and hop fields in `RB STATUS`.
- Host replay (NODE-006): `pio run -e native-replay`, `pio test -e native-test` (8 tests on four corpus
  fixtures). The real chain on the PC over the captures, host clock per sample.

Replay results on the corpus (same profile as the board, `program.exe --dir bench/sessions/2026-10-16-corpus-*`):

| Setting | 2800 | 3200 | 3300 | 3800 | noise (24 x 350 ms) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| band 2800-3800 | 12/12 accepted | **0/12** (all contrast weak) | 11/12 (one no candidate, 110 cm) | 12/12 | 0 candidates |
| single target 3,200 | - | 2/2 (90 cm, as the board's chirp cell) | 0/2 (contrast weak) | - | - |

1. **Equivalence of the front end.** With the board's baseline seeded from `RAW_SUMMARY baseline=`, the host
   amp feature equals the board's recorded amp column to 1 Strength16 (rounding) on all 48 captures. Without
   the seed the difference is a constant 20-290 per capture: the board's baseline is set at boot from the
   first 200 samples, inside the DC blocker's start transient, and then barely moves (quiet-gated tracker),
   so every boot carries its own offset (512, 460, 2,294 PCM in three captures). Harmless for detection
   (4-18 % of the amp limit at worst, nothing in the Goertzel path), but a boot artefact worth knowing.
2. **The plain 3,200 Hz chirp is off-bin for the band.** Bins sit at 2800 + k x 250; 3,200 lands between
   3050 and 3300, its energy splits and the contrast P75 falls below 30,500 in every capture, at every
   spacing. The board's chirp cells never showed this because they ran without `band=`. Consequence for the
   Node, which now listens in the band by default: a plain 3,200 Hz chirp (idle voice, SELFTEST, the 5a
   reference) is not accepted by a band-mode node. Either the band or `CHIRP_FREQUENCY_HZ` moves to a bin
   (3,300 is strong at every spacing measured today and on 2026-10-14) or the 5a reference is re-defined on a
   bin. Owner's call (decisions/2026-10-09-chirp-frequency-stays-3200.md has the revisit note).
3. Noise snippets give no detector candidate at all in 6 s of radio; the board saw about one candidate per
   2 s of radio in OBS. Short snippets (350 ms, marker at the start) under-represent onsets; longer captures
   need a longer firmware buffer or chained captures.

  stressor than the phone radio. Reasons are hypotheses (speaker spectrum).
