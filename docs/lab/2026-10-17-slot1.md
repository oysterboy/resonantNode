## Afternoon: the deafness had a cause, five boards, a hearing matrix, and a rest rule

- **The cause of the wandering deafness and the DMA drops: blocking serial output.** Only the Analyzer build had a
  serial TX buffer; on the Node every print waited for the 115200-baud UART. One 1,133-byte `RB STATUS` line held
  the loop about 80 ms and cost 10 dropped DMA buffers (2 prints in 60 s: 10 drops; 10 prints: 100). With the log on
  and two nodes talking, the stalls added up. Fix 0ede378: 16 KB TX buffer in every build. Afterwards: 0 dropped
  buffers in 70 s with six STATUS prints, 0 in a 10-minute E2-E3 run, and no deaf phase in any later run.
  (`bench/sessions/2026-10-17-pair-after-txfix`.) Timestamp backsteps rose to about one every 3 s since; harmless
  without drops, watched. Reviewed the same evening: the negative half of the 500 ms clock correction, driven by
  `delay(1)` polling against the 8 ms DMA period, not by the TX fix; parked as NODE-019 (roadmap-node.md).
- **Step 5c, five boards** (`bench/sessions/2026-10-17-selftest-e1-e4-e5`): E1's "dead amp" was a loose 5 V Dupont
  wire, E4 passes, E5's mic reads all zeros on its 3 m and 5 m (repeater) cables and works on a 1 m cable (cause not
  separated: E5's margin or those cables, E4 runs on a 4-5 m one). All five pass board, mic, quiet boot and amp
  (E5 on the short cable). The `own_chirp_suppressed` check fails whenever other Nodes are near: their answers land
  in its 300 ms echo window. E3's output sounds harsh to the owner; its own tone varies 2x between identical chirps
  and runs at 175,000 against 260,000-310,000 on the others, with the mic floor jumping along: a supply or contact
  issue on E3, not yet checked. E4 and E5 share hop rotation 0 (MAC % 5).
- **Hearing matrix** (`bench/sessions/2026-10-17-pairmatrix`, `tools/bench/pairmatrix.py`, one pair at a time, the
  others held silent): placement right to left E2 -70- E3 -70- E1 -40- E4 -49- E5 (cm), E2/E3 20 cm higher. Contact
  follows distance and the height step: 40 cm same height 259/259, 70 cm 30-35 of 36, 110-180 cm 0 or one-way.
  **E1 and E4 answered each other 518 times in 180 s**, a turn every 350 ms: the 400 ms refractory does not stop a
  chain on the bench (the host test's arithmetic said it would; the room tail and the close timing differ).
- **Rest rule** (decision `2026-10-17-two-node-exchange-chain-length.md`, owner; code 2ddf2b4, host tests
  `test_behavior_rest`): >= 4 valid network patterns in the field window, or 6 own emissions in 10 s, start a
  hard 10 s rest without answers or idle voice; only valid network verdicts count; same values on every node.
  Bench, E1-E4 pair (`bench/sessions/2026-10-17-rest-rule-E1E4`): 518 turns -> 59, eight rests, hearing unchanged.
- **Four Nodes together** with the rest rule, 10 min, unattended (`bench/sessions/2026-10-17-group4-rest-rule`,
  `tools/bench/nodegroup.py`): 29-43 emissions per minute for the group, steady; longest silences 18-20 s; 17 % of
  node-time resting; no DMA drops. The rule ends the endless chain, but four nodes never fall silent together: one is
  always out of rest and an idle call restarts things. For a quiet network: longer rest, lower field threshold or a
  longer field window. Contact: E1 <- E4 74 %, all other links 5-31 % (placement over 1 m, height step, E3 unstable).
